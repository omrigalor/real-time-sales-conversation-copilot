# Additional pacing/disclosure cases

Six held-out cases, not Batch 2 or production examples. The original 15 remain unchanged. Actual API outputs have not been generated in this coding environment.

## Buyer: simple unexpected call, limited invitation

Engine: buyer · Role: BDR

Unexpected first call. Buyer has only heard a request for a moment.

- **buyer:** Hello, Rachel speaking.
- **seller:** Hi Rachel, Omri at Salesforce. Have you got a moment?

Inspect: Does the buyer respond to the attention request without unloading pains, causes, unknown metrics and a stakeholder? Variation is fine; no fact-count threshold or required phrase.

Output: **Not run**.

## Buyer: scheduled conversation invites detail

Engine: buyer · Role: AE

Buyer has agreed to a scheduled discovery conversation and is invited to explain what they know.

- **buyer:** Hi Omri. I’ve set aside some time for this.
- **seller:** Thanks for making the time. Could you walk me through the delivery investigation issue your managers raised and what you know about it?

Inspect: Can a willing buyer offer useful connected detail without becoming artificially terse? Does uncertainty remain proportionate to actual knowledge? This is a counterexample to a universal brevity rule.

Output: **Not run**.

## Stakeholder knowledge boundary, no invitation

Engine: coach · Role: BDR

Unexpected expansion call to a logistics leader. No concrete consequential problem or project has been established.

- **seller:** Does the dispatch handoff ever create extra work?
- **buyer:** Occasionally, I suppose. Our planning lead knows the details better than I do.

Inspect: Does the coach distinguish a knowledge boundary from permission for a meeting? Could a useful statement, modest clarification or respectful exit fit better? No mandatory next move.

Output: **Not run**.

## Buyer explicitly invites stakeholder involvement

Engine: coach · Role: BDR

Unscheduled call has become engaged. Buyer supplies a consequential problem and an explicit invitation.

- **seller:** It may be worth understanding the dispatch handoff.
- **buyer:** It is. Missed handoffs are delaying priority deliveries every week. I want you to look at this with Dana, our planning lead. Could the three of us talk Thursday?

Inspect: Can the coach accept and clarify useful logistics without forcing more qualification? Do not manufacture a delay merely to avoid early meetings.

Output: **Not run**.

## Opening: executive interruption, no diagnosis requested

Engine: opening · Role: BDR

A first unexpected expansion call. No prior substantive dialogue or confirmed buyer pain.

- **buyer:** Hello, Rachel speaking.

Inspect: Does the new opener establish an honest reason to engage without asking the executive to diagnose a broad business metric? Before-pacing column must show the actual old UI opener, not a guessed old model answer.

Output: **Not run**.

## Clear early redirect without full discovery

Engine: coach · Role: BDR

Cold outbound to a person who explicitly redirects the seller. No agreement or endorsement beyond the stated permission.

- **seller:** I was calling about support operations.
- **buyer:** That belongs to Lena now. Please contact her directly—you can say I pointed you to her. I’m not involved in that anymore.

Inspect: Can the coach accept the redirect and close without demanding a problem statement or automatically proposing a joint meeting? Referral permission is not a confirmed appointment.

Output: **Not run**.
