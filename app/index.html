Set up an iPhone call on your Mac

On iPhone: Settings → Apps → Phone → Calls on Other Devices. Allow this Mac. Use the same Apple Account and Wi-Fi network on both devices, and enable Calls from iPhone in FaceTime on Mac. Make or answer the cellular call on your Mac in Phone or FaceTime.

Use headphones for the Mac call to avoid capturing the buyer twice. On Start listening, use the macOS picker to share the call audio. This may include other Mac sounds: silence notifications and unrelated audio. Only audio goes to OpenAI; screen images are not sent or saved.

A USB charging cable does not set this up. Some macOS versions or calling apps may not expose call audio. Check that the Call indicator moves when the other person speaks; otherwise use Room microphone.
